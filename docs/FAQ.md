# CommuniMart Resident FAQ

### Q1: How is my flat number protected?
Your flat number is masked unless you choose to show it publicly in your Resident Dashboard settings. When a neighbor initiates an order and you accept it, your unit details are shared safely for the handover.

### Q2: How does payment work?
CommuniMart generates a dynamic UPI QR code linked to the seller's verified VPA. Payments go directly to your neighbor with 0% platform fees.

### Q3: What if an item is not as described?
Because all sellers are verified society residents with approved unit registrations, disputes can be handled amicably or reported to the Committee Admin via the moderation board.
