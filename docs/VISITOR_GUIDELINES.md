# Society Visitor & Delivery Logistics

Procedures for external delivery personnel and guests entering Green Meadows Gated Community:

1. **Security Gate Check-in**: All non-residents must register at Gate 1 or Gate 2.
2. **Contactless Drop-box**: Deliveries left for neighbors can be deposited in the Clubhouse locker area.
3. **Emergency Numbers**: Gate 1 (+91 98765 43210), Gate 2 (+91 98765 43219).
